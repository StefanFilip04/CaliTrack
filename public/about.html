About Me

Calisthenics Athlete & Software Developer

My Calisthenics Journey

I started calisthenics 3 years ago, frustrated with the scattered, unstructured content on YouTube and social media. I wanted a clear path from beginner to advanced skills, with progressions that actually work.

Today, I've achieved skills like [Front Lever Pull-ups], [Planche], [Weighted Muscle-up], [90 degree Handstand Pushup] and other skills as such. But more importantly, I've learned that structured progression is the key to unlocking any skill.

Why I Built CaliTrack

I built CaliTrack to solve the problem I faced as a beginner: no clear roadmap. Most fitness apps focus on weights and machines, not bodyweight mastery. CaliTrack provides structured skill trees, progress tracking, and a workout builder designed specifically for calisthenics athletes.

Whether you're just starting or chasing advanced skills like Planche and Front Lever, this platform gives you the tools to track your journey.

My Philosophy

"Consistency over intensity. Progression over ego. Master the basics before chasing the advanced."

Calisthenics is a marathon, not a sprint. Small daily improvements compound into massive results over time. Track your progress, trust the process, and enjoy the journey.
